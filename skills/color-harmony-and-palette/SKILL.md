# Poster Color Harmony & Chromatic Discipline

## 1. The Core Problem: The "Clownish Rainbow" Anti-Pattern
An amateur design mistake is selecting 4-5 unrelated bright colors (e.g. cyan, neon green, bright yellow, coral pink, royal blue) and scattering them across text elements. This destroys visual cohesion, degrades brand credibility, and causes visual fatigue.

## 2. The 60-30-10 Editorial Chromatic Rule
Every professional poster palette must strictly adhere to three proportional roles:

1. **60% Dominant Canvas (Atmosphere & Negative Space)**:
   - Sets the mood and covers the largest visual area (Layer 0 background).
   - In dark-mode/night posters: Deep obsidian, midnight indigo (`#0A0E1A`), or charcoal.
   - In light-mode/print posters: Warm cream (`#F8F6F0`), parchment, or architectural gray.
   - *Never* use pure harsh 100% black (`#000000`) or blinding white.

2. **30% Structural Typographic Tone (Readability & Body Copy)**:
   - Used for primary typography, headlines, dates, venues, and structural dividers.
   - Must have maximum optical contrast against the 60% base (WCAG contrast > 7:1).
   - On dark canvases: Clean high-value off-white/cream (`#FFFFFF`, `#F0F4F8`, `#FFF8EE`).
   - On light canvases: Deep ink navy (`#0B132B`) or rich graphite (`#1A1A1A`).

3. **10% High-Impact Accent / Focal Pop (Strictly ONE Hue)**:
   - Reserved strictly for the single most important focal element (e.g., Grand Prize or CTA anchor).
   - Must be a single cohesive accent color derived directly from the theme or hero asset lighting:
     - *Cyber/Tech*: Electric Cyan (`#00F5D4`) OR Terminal Amber (`#FFB800`) — **pick one, never both**.
     - *Music/Night Fest*: Warm Amber Flame (`#FF8A3D`) OR Neon Coral (`#FF4D6D`).
     - *Eco/Bio*: Luminous Emerald (`#10B981`).

## 3. Strict Color Assignment Rules for Poster Text
- **Headline / Title**: 100% Structural Tone (White/Off-White on dark background) or Accent.
- **Organizer / Subtitle**: Muted structural tone (70% opacity / subtle tint of dominant).
- **Event Lineup & Dates**: Structural Tone (White/Cream). High legibility.
- **Grand Prize / CTA**: The **10% Accent Color**. This is where the accent earns maximum optical punch.
- **Rule**: NEVER assign different rainbow colors to consecutive lines of text. A poster should look designed with a unified 2-to-3 color ink print run.
