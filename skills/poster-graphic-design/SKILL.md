# Editorial Poster Composition & Swiss Typographic Design

## 1. The Cardinal Rule: Posters are NOT Web Dashboards
An event poster is a large-format **editorial art piece**, not a mobile phone screen or SaaS dashboard.
- **NEVER DRAW CONTAINER CARDS**: Do not wrap event names, dates, or prices in rounded rectangle boxes (`add_shape_rectangle`), pill badges, or colored card backgrounds.
- **NEVER USE WEB BUTTONS**: A Call-To-Action (CTA) on a poster is a typographic link (e.g. `REGISTER: site.example/join`), NOT an HTML submit button with a colored background pill.
- **TEXT SITS DIRECTLY ON THE ATMOSPHERE**: Typography integrates directly into the background art, separated by subtle lighting gradients, optical drop shadows, or fine technical hairline rules (1px width).

## 2. Mathematical Typographic Hierarchy
Follow a strict mathematical scale (e.g. 14pt, 18pt, 24pt, 36pt, 48pt, 96pt):
1. **Primary Entry Point (Headline / Event Title)**:
   - Size: `88pt - 104pt`
   - Weight: `ExtraBold (800)` or `Black (900)`
   - Placement: Upper third of the canvas (`y: 160 - 240`)
   - Leading/Tracking: Clean, intentional letter-spacing (+2 to +4 tracking).
2. **Secondary Level (Subtitle, Date, Grand Prize)**:
   - Size: `22pt - 26pt` (approximately 1/4 of headline size)
   - Accent color: Bright neon or warm amber to command secondary attention.
3. **Tertiary Level (Event Lineup & Logistics)**:
   - Size: `16pt - 20pt`
   - Lineup Formatting: Set as an elegant typographic ribbon separated by typographic glyphs:
     `BATTLE OF BANDS   //   HACKNIGHT   //   DJ NIGHT   //   OPEN MIC`
     or
     `ROBO COMBAT   •   AUTONOMOUS DRONES   •   NEURAL HACK`
4. **Quaternary / Metadata Level (Blurb, Organizer, Sub-footer)**:
   - Size: `12pt - 14pt`
   - Set in clean, neutral tones (#DCE5F5, #9C8FC2) with generous line breathing room.

## 3. Depth Z-Stacking Architecture
Cinematic depth is achieved by interleaving vector elements and raster cutouts:
- **Layer 0**: Full-bleed background illustration (covers 100% of the canvas).
- **Layer 1**: Ambient radial light spill (screen-blended color glow).
- **Layer 2**: Massive Headline Display Title (`88pt+`).
- **Layer 3**: 3D Hero Cutout Asset (with native alpha transparency). The upper crown of the hero cutout physically overlaps the lower base of the headline title letters by `30px - 50px`. This creates the optical illusion that the subject is breaking forward out of the canvas.
- **Layer 4**: Foreground procedural accents (twinkling starfield, particles, cosmic dust).
- **Layer 5**: Editorial typography (lineup, prize ribbon, dual-language venue, CTA link).
- **Layer 6**: Border rules, bleed margins, and pre-flight certification.
