# Institutional Brand & Event Context

## Institution Profile
- **Institution Name**: Apex Institute of Technology
- **Abbreviation**: AIT
- **Motto / Tagline**: Empowering Tomorrow Through Innovation Today
- **Location**: Tech Campus, Grand Avenue, Sector 5, Innovation City
- **Official Website**: www.apextech.edu

## Associated Clubs & Societies
- **Design & Creative Guild**: The premier digital arts and visual media society of AIT. Tagline: "Crafting Vision into Reality".
- **AI & Robotics Club (ARC)**: Center for autonomous robotics, AI hackathons, and machine learning research. Tagline: "Code the Future".
- **Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell)**: Fostering startup culture and angel investment showcases. Tagline: "Ideas to Impact".

## Brand Design System

### Color Palette
- **Primary Navy**: `#0B132B` (deep background, primary text on light)
- **Tech Teal**: `#00F5D4` (accents, highlights, active elements)
- **Electric Cyan**: `#00BBF9` (secondary highlights, subtle glows)
- **Vibrant Magenta**: `#F72585` (key event highlights, callouts)
- **Pure White**: `#FFFFFF` (clean text, contrast backgrounds)
- **Cool Grey**: `#E0E1DD` (subtle borders, muted body text)
- **Warning Amber**: `#FEE440` (badges, dates, badges of honor)

### Typography Rules
- **Headline Font**: `Outfit, "Montserrat", sans-serif` (Bold 700 / ExtraBold 800)
- **Subheadline Font**: `Inter, "Segoe UI", sans-serif` (SemiBold 600)
- **Body & Metadata Font**: `Inter, system-ui, sans-serif` (Regular 400 / Medium 500)
- **Monospace / Code**: `JetBrains Mono, monospace` (for tech metrics, terminal vibes)

### Brand Rules & Usage
1. **Verbatim Text**: All names, dates, venues, website URLs, and club titles must be reproduced verbatim without truncation or alteration.
2. **Logo Placement**: 
   - Apex Institute of Technology logo (`college_logo.png` / `college_logo.svg`) must appear in the top header or prominent sponsor block.
   - Minimum rendered width: 120px.
   - Minimum clear space padding: 20px on all sides.
   - Must maintain original aspect ratio without distortion.
3. **Legibility & Contrast**:
   - Minimum contrast ratio for text over background is 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).
   - Minimum font size for metadata/body is 14pt (approx. 24px at 300 DPI).
4. **Layout Safety**:
   - Keep a minimum 40px margin from all canvas edges for essential text and logos.
